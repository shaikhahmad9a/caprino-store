Caprino is being imagined around a simple idea: buying fresh milk should feel reassuring, transparent and easy. We want product details, sourcing, handling and storage guidance to be clear—not hidden in fine print.

GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Helpful information for a confident first order.

Is Caprino currently taking orders?

No. This is a visual sample only. It is not connected to a real inventory, payment system or delivery operation.

Where will you deliver?

The proposed first service area is Mumbai. Exact pin codes and delivery capacity must be confirmed before launch.

How will freshness be protected?

The business must verify supplier processes, cold-chain requirements, packaging and safe handling instructions before selling.

Can I choose a delivery slot?

The planned store will support date and slot selection, but only after real delivery capacity is connected.
